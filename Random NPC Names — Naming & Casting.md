## Naming conventions

### Wode elves

Elves treat gendered traits as changeable expression rather than fixed determinants of procreation. Wode elves may adopt a new name or a variant when their presentation changes: Llyandros might become Llyandra.

### High elves

Names are poetic attempts to capture fleeting, sometimes contradictory impulses or emotions. They may refer to nature, but are not literal descriptions or deliberately heroic titles. Echoes of Autumn Heat is an existing supporting NPC.

### Polders

Vasloria’s polders use sophisticated-sounding first names and surnames formed from common compound words. Langston Pikecatcher is an existing example.

### Other ancestries

Specific naming rules are not recorded on the original names page. Use the database rather than inventing a new convention.

## Quick NPC record

- Name, ancestry, age, and gender where relevant.
- Role and home location.
- Appearance and scent.
- Voice, distinctive behavior, and quirk.
- Motivation, flaw, and why they might help or deny aid.
- First session appearance and relationships.
