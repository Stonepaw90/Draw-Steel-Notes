## Shelter

A layered city ruled by the State and the Father of the Nation, with the Maiden as its revered benefactor. Jobs, social security, Friday feasts, and Saturday Champion Games underpin its public promise of prosperity. Beneath that promise lie disappearances, manipulated rivalries, segregation, and life-creation experiments.

### The Glay Construction

Dreary slums named in the campaign pitch. Rumors of ghouls, framed prisoners, and gang violence reveal the city’s corruption. Glay Construction, the Thieves Guild, and Blue HQ also appear as location prompts in the setting notes; exact geography is not yet established.

### Brenda’s Tavern / the Inn

Starting point of Session 1 and the arson target of Session 2. A social hub for Zaar, Brenda, the heroes, and Zumi’s bread. The notes mention a designated combat area and a private “Talk it out” room.

The general tavern description has loud conversations, sloshing beer, round tables, a fireplace, and firewood smells. It features Old Fenra and Darzok Reed; whether it describes this same tavern is unconfirmed.

### The Heart

Civic center where new arrivals apply for citizenship. A proposed venue for wedding celebrations in finale planning. A labeled map remains in the original Setting page.

### Champion’s Place / the Arena

Public fights settle rivalries and offer the chance to request a boon from the Maiden. The historical destruction of the Dockworkers Union by the Thieves Guild led to revised rules. One-shot notes include duels, executions, honor battles, and service obligations for surrender.

The arena is a possible wedding/finale venue, not a settled location for the conclusion.

### Museum and vaults

One’s shield is guarded by a powerful magical ward. Fealti’s ring is recorded beneath the museum. Recovering the shield can strengthen Justice; methods in the quest notes include theft, help from Awe, or obtaining it through the games.

### Jail

Warm and well kept, with the smell of bread, bunked cots, a latrine, and tall iron bars that humans can detect as magical. An adjacent cell holds Orc-speaking raiders; Kalliak is named in the notes. This is a one-shot location, not assumed part of the six-session sequence.

### Old city tunnels and ruins

Routes beneath modern Shelter pass basements and remains of earlier layers. Session 3 follows a distressed ghoul downhill for about an hour into this underground world.

### Northern mine and laboratory

A mine collapse brings rescuers to injured dwarves and ghouls in Session 4. A damaged lab, green light, and warnings of a volatile experiment lead toward the Maiden and Awe’s work creating or reforming life.

### House of Clan Lodgy and library

Focus of Session 5’s diplomacy and historical investigation. Anna and Lurza connect the family’s power to the future of Zaar’s relationship and the dwarf divide. Finale planning recaps a library visit; the exact library name is unrecorded.

### Rebellion meeting places

The quest notes place rebels in the slums and northern city. Survivors of arena violence and manufactured racial conflict organize against the Maiden.

## Beyond the walls

- **Burned wilderness:** The Weeping Wyrm destroys living nature outside Shelter.
- **Farmers’ excursions:** Zumi’s bread thread leads to farmers tending land beyond the walls; this is an optional quest thread.
- **Dramhail Pass:** The party plans a fort here after Session 5. Finale notes consider moving the wedding to the pass.
- **Mountain halls:** Associated with King Dramhail and the early stone dwarves; names and precise locations remain unrecorded.
