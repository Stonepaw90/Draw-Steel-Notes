## Story focus

The heroes meet in Brenda’s Tavern. A confrontation around Zaar’s relationship with Lurza brings the stone/flesh divide into immediate, personal focus.

## Recorded developments

- Five dwarves arrive and a fight breaks out in the designated combat zone.
- Theodora puts a barbed spring in an antagonist’s nose; Warden is punched through a wall.
- Zaar tells the party about Lurza.
- Brenda is the barmaid played by Sylvia.
- Peter arrives, described as chased from hell and Swedish-speaking.
- The following morning brings Zumi/Zuni and his bread.

These developments are recorded in the latter part of the session notes; the longer dialogue above them is preparation rather than a verbatim play transcript.

## Prepared scenes and hooks

- Two cloaked poster-makers advertise the resistance. A chase can become a montage; catching them reveals two children.
- Citizens mention disappearances, arrested dissenters, separated enclaves, and students recruited by the School of Illusions.
- Zumi’s remarkable bread points toward farming beyond the city walls.
- Five flesh dwarves threaten Zaar over his relationship with Lurza Lodgy. The private “Talk it out” room and the tavern’s no-bar-fights rule shape the confrontation.

## Cast and connections

Zaar ↔ Lurza: a relationship threatened by prejudice. Gruin and Drokki experience the divide from opposite ancestries. Warden connects tavern intimidation to later finale imagery.
