## Story focus

An attack on the inn is staged to look like flesh-dwarf violence. The heroes must save people, pursue the culprit, and uncover deliberate manipulation of the stone/flesh conflict.

## Prepared opening

Breakfast at the inn: Sloan prays to a horned Maiden icon; humans sense a brief supernatural presence. Cedric, a sleepy and profane State guard, arrives. A Hakaan praises Drokki’s brawling, and a polder thanks the heroes for the prior night.

## Encounter sequence in the notes

1. Unsalted food and a burning smell hint at danger.
2. A fire spreads; a montage determines how much of the encounter area remains ablaze.
3. A red-insignia dwarf flees. Breaking the illusion reveals a fire Elementalist rather than the apparent flesh dwarf.
4. The Elementalist uses portals and summons undead from beneath the city. This reminds Drokki of his own portal ability.
5. An encoded note becomes crucial evidence; vowels are numbered 1–5. If the attacker drops it while escaping, she returns for it.

## Central clue

The instructions order the attacker to impersonate “Gunnar,” a flesh dwarf, burn the heroes’ tavern visibly, turn Gruin against flesh dwarves, and prevent the party from healing the deliberately created divide. The attacker must not be captured.

This changes the conflict from ordinary prejudice to organized provocation.

## Recorded continuity

[[Session 4 — Demigods in the Mines]]’s notes refer back to the Elementalist’s death and the tavern still standing. [[Session 3 — Ghouls and Demons]] follows the undead underground. The Session 2 notes themselves remain largely encounter preparation; exact fire damage, clue recovery, and capture details are not separately recorded.
