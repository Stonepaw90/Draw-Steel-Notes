## Story focus

The trail of undead leads below Shelter. A distressed ghoul makes the creatures’ humanity—or its absence—a central question.

## Sequence preserved in the notes

- Enter the hole and track the undead.
- Find a ghoul sobbing over a heart it has carved. It possesses heart and soul, but no mind.
- It panics and flees through branching tunnels, looking back over its shoulder.
- Other undead threaten the pursuing heroes, including one that lunges and tries to bite.
- The route slopes downward for about an hour. Side passages reveal basements; the tunnel crosses ruins of the older city.
- The fleeing ghoul reaches a dead end and cowers.

The source is scene-oriented rather than a complete recap. Do not assume all prepared beats occurred exactly as written.

## Wider continuity

Later campaign feedback mentions a hostile ghoul holding pen and a negotiation framed as fighting an entire city or selling a soul. The notes do not securely assign these details to this session, so they remain broader campaign context rather than a reconstructed Session 3 outcome.

## Connections

The carved heart foreshadows the demigods’ division of heart, mind, and soul. The old city ruins connect the investigation to Shelter’s layered history. The underground route leads toward the mines and laboratory.
