## Central stakes

Zaar and Lurza’s wedding could become an act of unity or a public disaster engineered by the Maiden. The party must weigh family tensions, demigod influence, and their own commitments while choosing how to confront Fealti.

## Preparation and downtime

- Recap the meeting with Lurza, library, and Anna.
- Resolve negotiation rewards and requests to the Grand Matriarch.
- Decide what to do with the listening key/coin and Awe’s invitation. The earlier notes call the device a key; finale planning calls it a coin. Confirm whether these are the same object.
- Consider keep construction at Dramhail Pass, workers, land, wealth, and rooms. Project rules remain in the original finale notes.
- Zaar works with Gruin; Lurza works with Isolde on wedding planning.

## Wedding options in the source

- An arena celebration with the Father of the Nation, projections, shows, and armed entourages.
- The Maiden officiating, providing food, and presenting the event as her benevolence.
- Celebrations in the Heart.
- Moving the wedding to Dramhail Pass.
- A three-month wait, with the heroes choosing how to spend the time.

These locations and timings are alternatives, not a finalized schedule.

## Intrigue threads

- Elders and guards on both sides escalate fear and armament.
- A stone textile ambassador faces tariffs, bodyguard costs, and unsafe trade routes.
- A nearby elder’s locked chest may reveal smuggling and empty gold pouches.
- Commercial portals are regulated by the void elementalists’ guild.
- Sha/Xh’a, recently escaped from hell, recognizes X’arnath and reports new souls with improbable names appearing on ledgers.

## Possible confrontation

The notes consider magical influence on Zaar, violence at the wedding, an assassin, and an ambush arranged by the heroes. One draft has Zaar killed with Warden’s hammer; this is a possible scene, not a recorded death.

Another approach uses handcuffs forged in dragon fire. The notes contain different test-order ideas for restraining the Maiden; finalize the mechanics before play rather than treating either draft as settled.

A fight with Fealti would require surviving several rounds; Justice and Lurza may assist. Awe may perform a ritual freeing souls, opening further conversation about the ghouls. These are planned possibilities only.

## Decisions still open

- Final venue, timing, and wedding agreement.
- How Fealti intervenes and what fair warning the heroes receive.
- Whether restraint, negotiation, resistance, or direct combat is the main resolution.
- Which allies actually arrive and what the heroes must do to earn their help.
- Whether the ending heals the divide, protects the created beings, and establishes a new future.
