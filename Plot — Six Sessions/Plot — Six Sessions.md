## Campaign throughline

In Shelter, the State promises prosperity while engineered division keeps stone and flesh dwarves apart. The heroes’ investigation leads from tavern violence and arson to underground experiments, the demigods, and the possibility of healing the divide through Zaar and Lurza’s relationship.

The Maiden, Fealti, seeks worship and power. One represents justice and unity; Awe represents nature and the soul. Their conflict turns the party’s local choices into a struggle over freedom, creation, and the city’s future.
