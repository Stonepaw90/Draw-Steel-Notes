## Story focus

Diplomacy with Clan Lodgy and historical inquiry give the party tools to challenge the dwarven divide. Zaar and Lurza’s future becomes entangled with family power, commerce, and the party’s plans beyond Shelter.

## Historical account learned

The high elf historian says King Dramhail remained in his mountain halls while flesh dwarves followed Justice to found Shelter. Caramin’s writings suggest some stone dwarves participated independently of Dramhail.

Flesh dwarves hollowed out the foundation with hands and songs. Stone dwarves later fled a flying, possibly fire-breathing beast, joined the rebuilding of failing lower levels, and received the upper city.

## Recorded party actions and plans

- Built a sex dungeon for Zaar and Lurza.
- Stole and read Lurza’s journal.
- Planned to adopt a stone dwarf and a flesh dwarf.
- Planned a fort at Dramhail Pass.
- Hero wealth is recorded as 3, following money from negotiation and payment from Fealti.

Finale planning recaps the session as meetings with Lurza, the library, and Anna Lodgy.

## Consequences and questions

The party has made concrete investments in cross-dwarf relationships and a future outside the city. Negotiation opens possibilities for land, workers, and a keep, but the fort is a plan rather than a completed construction.

The final agreement, wedding date, family vote, and journal contents are not fully captured in this session’s recap.
