## Story focus

A mine disaster reveals the Maiden and Awe’s experiments. The party encounters powers far greater than the tavern gangs and must decide how to respond to creation, coercion, and the possibility of alliance.

## Prepared rescue and discovery

- A dream featuring blue eyes warns of the demigods’ activities.
- A nighttime collapse in the northern mine brings dust, cries for help, dwarves, and ghouls needing rescue.
- An injured dwarf explains that an experiment went wrong; prejudice can complicate accepting healing from a stone dwarf.
- Green light and a ruined laboratory lead to Awe supporting stone while the Maiden rescues creatures.
- Their disagreement is whether to create anew or improve existing attempts; they speak of needing their brother.

## The demigods’ positions

Fealti claims she wants a family and provides housing, feasts, and public games. Awe claims participation lets him improve the beings’ lives and provide a love of nature rather than obsession with Fealti. Awe warns that Fealti has never been so powerful.

The Maiden refers to the Elementalist’s death and predicts that the heroes will never heal the dwarves’ divide.

## Recorded developments

- The Maiden transforms a ghoul into an animated ghoul in front of the party.
- She asks the heroes to connect her with Aurumvas.
- The party receives a key that listens to them and can be flipped to speak with her.
